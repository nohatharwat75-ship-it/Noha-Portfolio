About

Professor Noha Tharwat Abou El-Khier is a full professor at the Faculty of Medicine, Mansoura University — one of Egypt's most prominent academic medical institutions — where she has built a career defined by scientific rigor, interdisciplinary collaboration, and unwavering commitment to public health. She progressed through every academic rank over more than two decades, earning full professorship in 2021 in the Department of Medical Microbiology & Immunology.

Her research portfolio spans 51 peer-reviewed publications indexed in Scopus, PubMed, and Google Scholar, tackling among the most pressing challenges in contemporary infectious disease: antimicrobial resistance, biofilm-mediated virulence, quorum sensing gene expression, and the molecular epidemiology of nosocomial pathogens. Her work has appeared in International Orthopaedics, Leukemia & Lymphoma, Journal of Global Antimicrobial Resistance, Frontiers in Cellular and Infection Microbiology, and The Pediatric Infectious Disease Journal, among others.

Beyond the bench, Professor Abou El-Khier is an institution-builder. She reviews for more than 20 international journals, sits on the Institutional Research Board, leads infection control teams across multiple Mansoura University hospitals, and mentors the next generation through active MSc and MD supervision. A bilingual communicator fluent in Arabic and English, she is a sought-after speaker on scientific writing, bibliometrics, and AI-powered research — most recently delivering invited lectures at the 31st ESMM Conference and the 12th World Congress on Antibiotics in Paris (2025).
